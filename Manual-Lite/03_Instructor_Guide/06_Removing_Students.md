# Removing Students

Removing a student takes that student off the course roster. You do it when someone drops the class or was enrolled by mistake. Unlike bulk enrollment, removal is an instructor action you can perform yourself.

## Prerequisites

- A course assigned to you. See [Creating a Course](02_Creating_a_Course.md).
- The student is currently enrolled in that course.

## Removing a student from the roster

1. Open the **Instructor** entry in the left sidebar.
2. On the **My Courses** tab, open the course.
3. Select the **Students** sub-tab.
4. Find the student's row and click the **Remove** (trash) button in that row's actions.

**What you should see:** The student disappears from the roster. The enrollment link between the student and the course is deleted.

## What removal does and does not do

| Effect | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Course enrollment | Removed. The student no longer sees the course or its labs. |
| Lab completions and flags | Preserved. Removal does not delete the student's progress records. |
| Re-joining later | The student can re-join with the invite code, or an administrator can re-enroll them. |

!!! note
    Removal only severs the enrollment. The student's completions and flag history remain in the platform, so the work is not lost if the student re-joins.

!!! tip
    To re-add a removed student, share the course invite code again. See [Generating and Sharing Invite Codes](04_Generating_and_Sharing_Invite_Codes.md).

Usernames and emails on the Students sub-tab show in full unless you turn on Privacy Mode, the optional sidebar toggle that is off by default. To review a student's per-lab progress before removing them, see [Monitoring Student Progress](09_Monitoring_Student_Progress.md).
