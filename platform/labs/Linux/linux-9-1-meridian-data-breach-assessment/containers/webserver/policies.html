Company Policies

POL-001: Data Classification Policy

All data handled by Meridian Financial Group must be classified into one of the following categories:

  • Public: Information intended for public disclosure (marketing materials, press releases)
  • Internal: General business information for employee use (intranet content, procedures)
  • Confidential: Sensitive business information (financial reports, strategic plans)
  • PII (Personally Identifiable Information): Client and employee personal data (SSN, account numbers, addresses)

All databases containing PII must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Access to PII data requires department head approval and is logged by the compliance monitoring system.

POL-002: Data Retention Policy

Data retention periods are defined by data classification:

  • Public data: No retention limit
  • Internal data: 3 years after creation
  • Confidential data: 7 years after creation
  • PII data: Minimum 7 years per SEC/FINRA requirements; purge within 30 days of retention expiry

Backup files must follow the same retention schedule. Legacy backups from decommissioned systems must be archived for 90 days and then securely purged.

POL-003: Acceptable Use Policy

Company computing resources are provided for business purposes. Employees must:

  • Use strong, unique passwords for all systems
  • Report suspected security incidents to the IT department immediately
  • Not install unauthorized software on company devices
  • Not access systems or data outside their authorized scope
  • Lock workstations when leaving their desk

POL-004: Backup and Recovery Policy

All production databases are backed up daily at 02:00 UTC. Backups are stored on the NetBackup appliance with AES-256 encryption. Legacy backup procedures:

  • Legacy SQL dumps are retained for 90 days post-migration
  • Legacy backups are stored in the archive directory on the intranet server
  • Access to backup archives requires IT department authorization
  • All backup credentials use application-level service accounts with limited privileges

POL-005: Incident Response Policy

In the event of a suspected data breach or security incident:

  • Report immediately to IT Director (James Park, x4201) and CCO (Laura Chen, x4105)
  • Do not attempt to remediate on your own
  • Preserve all evidence. do not delete logs or modify files
  • Document the timeline of events
  • External breach notification must occur within 72 hours per state law requirements
