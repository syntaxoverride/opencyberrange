About Crestline

Founded in 1996 by Margaret Crestline and Nathan Cole, Crestline Financial Services has grown from a boutique advisory firm in Charlotte, NC to a full-service wealth management company with offices in Charlotte, Atlanta, and Washington, D.C. We manage over $2.4 billion in assets for more than 1,200 client families.


Our philosophy is simple: your wealth should work as hard as you do. Every client receives a dedicated advisor, a custom investment policy statement, and quarterly performance reviews. We are a fiduciary. always.
