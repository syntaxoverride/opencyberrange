About Vanguard Logistics

Founded in 2009 by Marcus Chen and Dana Reeves, Vanguard Logistics has grown from a regional carrier into a full-service logistics provider operating across the southeastern United States. Headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, our team of 320 employees manages over 15,000 shipments per month.


We are ISO 9001 certified and maintain a 99.4% on-time delivery rate. Our technology-first approach integrates with major ERP and e-commerce platforms for seamless supply chain visibility.
