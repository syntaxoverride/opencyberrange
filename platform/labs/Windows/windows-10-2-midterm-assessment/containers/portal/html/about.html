About Pinnacle Legal Group

Founded in 1994, Pinnacle Legal Group has grown from a small corporate law practice into one of the region's most respected mid-size firms. With 89 attorneys and professional staff across two offices, we provide comprehensive legal services to businesses ranging from emerging startups to Fortune 500 companies.

Our commitment to client service, combined with deep industry expertise, has earned us recognition from Chambers USA, Best Lawyers, and the National Law Journal. We pride ourselves on delivering practical, results-oriented counsel that helps our clients navigate complex legal challenges.

Our History

Pinnacle was founded by Margaret Thompson and Robert Kessler with a vision of building a law firm that combined big-firm talent with personalized client service. Over three decades, the firm has expanded its practice areas, opened a second office, and built a reputation for excellence in corporate transactions, intellectual property, and commercial litigation.

Community Involvement

Pinnacle Legal Group is committed to giving back. Our attorneys contribute over 5,000 pro bono hours annually through partnerships with legal aid organizations and the City Bar Association's volunteer program.
